Frequently asked questions answered

How do I play?

Connect each pair of matching numbers with a path. Paths cannot cross, and every cell on the board must be filled. Use Undo if you want to take back a move.

How is scoring calculated?

Your score is 10,000 divided by your time in seconds, then rounded. Faster finishes score higher. Hints do not change the number, but they are saved with the result.

What does Hint do?

Hint fills in a correct stretch of path for you. Each use is counted on that game. A finish with no hints is a clean solve — the Leaderboard can show those separately.

Where can I see past games?

After you log in, open History from your profile menu. Filter by week, month, or all time, and by grid size. Share a result as Private, or let anyone with the link view it.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest. Log in or sign up to save scores, open History and the Leaderboard, leave a review, and share a result.

Are there difficulty levels?

Difficulty scales with board size. Larger grids make longer, tighter puzzles.

How do I start a game?

Choose Width and Height from 7 to 11, then press PLAY. 9×9 is selected by default.

Can I leave a review?

Yes. On Reviews, rate NumberLink from 1 to 5 and add a comment. You need an account to read and post.
